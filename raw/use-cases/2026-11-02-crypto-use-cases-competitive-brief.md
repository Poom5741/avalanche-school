## Part 1: Real use cases in 2025–2026

### 1.1 Perp/derivatives trading — Hyperliquid

**What it is.** Hyperliquid is a decentralized exchange for perpetual futures ("perps" — leveraged bets on price moves that never expire) running on its own purpose-built L1 blockchain. **Architecture:** two halves in one network — **HyperCore**, the on-chain order-book trading engine (up to ~25x leverage), and **HyperEVM**, an EVM-compatible environment launched in early 2025 so other apps can build around the exchange ([bitsgap.com](https://bitsgap.com/blog/what-is-hyperliquid-hyperevm-and-hype-explained), [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11972709-what-is-hyperliquid-the-app-chain-perp-dex)). Trading is gasless for users.

**Volume milestones (dated):**
- Weekly volume grew from ~$13B in Q4 2024 to an average ~$47B in H1 2025 ([oakresearch.io, Aug 2025](https://oakresearch.io/en/reports/protocols/hyperliquid-hype-s1-2025-activity-report))
- Record single day: **$17.7B traded May 21, 2025** ([newsbtc via TradingView, May 27, 2025](https://www.tradingview.com/news/newsbtc:7dc1b4404094b:0-here-s-why-hyperliquid-hit-new-ath-at-39-and-why-it-could-continue/))
- Q2 2025: **$648B quarterly volume, $86.6M revenue** ([Binance Square, Aug 27, 2025](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/28907391094897))
- Full-year 2025: **~$633B processed**; on-chain DEX share of perp trading tripled from **6.42% → 24.3%** during 2025 ([VanEck, late 2025](https://www.vaneck.com/pl/en/blog/digital-assets/exploring-hyperliquid-redefining-derivatives-trading/)); Hyperliquid ended 2025 with **~56% of all decentralized perp volume** and **>$650M revenue (#4 in all of crypto)**, at times ~70% of the category ([Bankless, Dec 10, 2025](https://www.bankless.com/read/hyperliquid-the-year-perps-caught-fire); [Oak Research](https://oakresearch.io/en/reports/protocols/hyperliquid-hype-s1-2025-activity-report))
- As of **April 2026**: 30-day perp volume **>$180B — more than every other on-chain venue combined** ([Bitsgap](https://bitsgap.com/blog/what-is-hyperliquid-hyperevm-and-hype-explained)). UNCERTAIN: a Hyperliquid X post (late 2026) cited **$2.6B open interest in RWA perps** (tokenized stocks/commodities traded 24/7) — [x.com/HyperliquidX](https://x.com/HyperliquidX).

**The HYPE token story.** Genesis airdrop **Nov 29, 2024**: 310M HYPE (~31% of supply) to ~94K wallets, no VC allocation — the largest airdrop ever, worth ~$12B at peak ([ryder.id, Jul 2026](https://ryder.id/blogs/post/hyperliquid-hype-one-year-after-the-12b-airdrop); [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15039718-hyperliquid-airdrop-hype-genesis-facts-and-season-2-status)). HYPE hit an **ATH of $97.96 on Sept 23, 2026** ([cryptoticker.io, Oct 25, 2026](https://cryptoticker.io/en/prediction/hyperliquid-price-prediction/)); market cap reached **$18.3B (rank #11 on CMC) by Sept 10, 2025** ([Binance Square](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/29494642014833)). Cumulative protocol fees: **>$1.51B as of Aug 2026** ([SSRN paper, Aug 27, 2026](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/7367700.pdf?abstractid=7367700&mirid=1)). A spot HYPE ETF absorbed **1.04% of market cap in its first 10 trading days (June 2026)** ([Binance Square, Jun 3, 2026](https://www.binance.com/fr-AF/square/post/330364743193361)).

**Why it's the fastest-growing venue:** it proved a single killer app can BE the chain — revenue funds the token via buybacks, no gas for traders, CEX-like speed with self-custody. **Avalanche connection/comparison:** Hyperliquid is not built on Avalanche — it's the strongest evidence that app-specific chains can win huge (validating Avalanche's appchain thesis) while simultaneously demonstrating Avalanche *lost* the trading venue race (see §2.4 economics comparison).

### 1.2 Memecoin trading & pump.fun

**What it is.** pump.fun (launched **Jan 19, 2024**) is a Solana "launchpad": anyone mints a memecoin in under a minute on a bonding curve (price rises with buyers; liquidity migrates to a DEX at graduation — designed to deter rug pulls) ([blofin.com](https://blofin.com/en/academy/education/pumpfun/what-is-pump-fun); [cryptonews.com, Mar 12, 2026](https://cryptonews.com/news/pump-fun-solana-revenue-billion-expansion-ethereum-monad/)).

**Numbers (dated):** ~**12M tokens created** and **>22M lifetime unique users** by March 2026; at the late-2024 peak it was ~**62% of all daily Solana transactions** ([Cryptonews/Yahoo, Mar 12, 2026](https://cryptonews.com/news/pump-fun-solana-revenue-billion-expansion-ethereum-monad/)). Revenue: **$321M in 2024 → $664M in 2025** ([Binance Square, early 2026](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/303249257326977)); crossed **$1B cumulative revenue — Solana's first billion-dollar app — reported March 12, 2026** ([Cryptonews/Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/pump-fun-solana-first-1b-130219262.html)); it briefly out-earned Hyperliquid ($33.7M vs $32.7M over 30 days) ([KuCoin, 2026](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/pump-fun-surpasses-hyperliquid-in-monthly-revenue-for-first-time-since-april-2025)). Reality check: **~98.5% of tokens never finish the curve** (go to zero) ([Cryptonews, Mar 2026](https://cryptonews.com/news/pump-fun-solana-revenue-billion-expansion-ethereum-monad/)). In Q1 2026 pump.fun alone generated **$123M revenue** ([99Bitcoins via TradingView, 2026](https://www.tradingview.com/news/99Bitcoins:3334ff396094b:0-solana-tops-all-blockchains-in-dapp-revenue-for-ninth-straight-quarter/)).

**Equivalents on other chains:** **four.meme** on BNB Chain (77K+ tokens since 2024; daily volume briefly spiked $5M→$400M+; ~$2.97B volume cited Aug 2026) ([bingx.com, Apr 2025](https://bingx.com/en/learn/article/what-are-the-best-memecoin-launchpads-to-know-in-2025); [medium.com](https://medium.com/coinmonks/four-meme-guide-api-and-trading-platforms-873160bf5d58); [streetinsider, Aug 16, 2026](https://www.streetinsider.com/MarketMediaWire/Memecoin+Launchpad+Comparison%3A+MemeToro+vs.+Four.meme+vs.+Pump.fun+%E2%80%94+Who+Leads+BNB+Chain+in+2026/26933593.html)); **Clanker** on Base (noted as Base's main launchpad, though smaller) ([cryptonews, Mar 2026](https://cryptonews.com/news/pump-fun-solana-revenue-billion-expansion-ethereum-monad/)). **On Avalanche:** no pump.fun-scale launchpad exists; small-scale activity like the SocialFi app **The Arena** and the mid-2025 "Avalanche meme renaissance" (KET, etc.) peaked in summer 2025 at a fraction of Solana volumes ([dextools.io, mid-2025](https://info.dextools.io/avalanches-meme-projects-leading-the-charge/); [defillama.com/chain/avalanche, Nov 2, 2026](https://defillama.com/chain/avalanche)). Community threads repeatedly ask for a pump.fun equivalent with no dominant answer ([reddit r/Avax](https://www.reddit.com/r/Avax/comments/1f50uy9/does_avalanche_have_a_memecoin_launchpad_like/)).

### 2.6 Honest criticisms

## Curriculum takeaways (facts → framing)

1. **2025–2026 crypto revenue is dominated by trading and speculation** — perps (Hyperliquid: $633B in 2025) and memecoin launchpads (pump.fun: $1B cumulative) — plus the fast-growing "boring" rails: tokenized treasuries ($15B, Oct 2026) and stablecoins ($295B, Nov 2026).
2. **The winning pattern is "app = chain"** — Hyperliquid, FIFA, GUNZ, Tempo all launched purpose-built chains. Avalanche's architecture bet on exactly this future; its challenge is that the biggest appchain (Hyperliquid) and the biggest institutional chain (FIFA's is yes-Avalanche, but most finance RWA is Ethereum) — the middle of the market (retail DeFi, memecoins) went to Solana/Base.
3. **Avalanche's scoreboard, late 2026:** strongest-ever institutional pipeline (FIFA/World Cup, NYSE, Apollo) alongside its weakest-ever consumer metrics (TVL #13, stablecoins down). Both are true; a fair curriculum should teach both.
