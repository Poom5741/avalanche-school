## Corrections to apply everywhere

1. **Validator max balance attribution (most important technical error):** "Since the Shanghai upgrade, validators can hold up to 2,048 ETH" → change to **Pectra (May 2025, EIP-7251)**. Shanghai/Capella enabled withdrawals only. This is exactly the kind of fact learners repeat to devs — it must be right.
2. **Tokenized treasuries:** replace "$9–10B across ~60 products" with "**~$14.3B across 119 products (rwa.xyz, Nov 2, 2026)**"; drop the uncertainty flag. Also update "BUIDL is the largest product" → leadership flipped among USDY/USYC/BUIDL (~$2.1–2.4B each); Ondo is the largest platform (~$3.1B).
3. **Stablecoin peak:** "peaked ~$322B May 2026" → "**peaked ~$316B on May 20, 2026**" per DefiLlama API.
4. **Validator count:** "~870k validators" → "**~881k validators (Sept 30, 2026)**, down from 1.1M due to Pectra consolidation" — this also preempts a learner question about falling validator counts.
5. **Next Bitcoin halving:** "~March–April 2028" → "**~mid-April 2028 (est. Apr 8–14)**". Also fix the code citation: `MAX_MONEY` is in `src/consensus/amount.h` (consensus.h no longer defines it); the 210,000 interval constant (`nSubsidyHalvingInterval`) is set in `src/chainparams.cpp`.
6. **Scroll:** keep as an example but add a caveat: "still operating as of Nov 2026 but in decline (TVL −98.5%, exchange delistings, moving to a permissioned network)." Optionally note Starknet's post-quantum L1 research (non-binding vote; remains an Ethereum L2).
7. **Minor:** the ~13-minute finality figure is sound arithmetic (2 epochs × 6.4 min = 12.8 min) but isn't printed on ethereum.org; keep "~13 min" as derived, or cite the staking FAQ's "deposits recognized in around 13 minutes."

**Bottom line:** the brief is broadly reliable — every mechanism claim (gas, 1559, PoS, finality, rollups, ERCs, 7702) checked out against primary sources. The errors are concentrated in **time-sensitive figures and one upgrade attribution**: the Shanghai→Pectra mix-up (#2) and the tokenized-treasury total (#9) are the two that must be fixed before teaching.
