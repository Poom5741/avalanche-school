## Overview

Strip away the headlines and 2025–2026 crypto usage comes down to six things people actually do: trade with leverage (perps), gamble on memecoins, swap and lend in DeFi, hold tokenized traditional assets (RWA), pay with stablecoins, and play games. This article is the anchor for Module 3 — the verified usage map as of **November 2, 2026** — plus the three framing lessons the curriculum draws from it. Two things are true at the same time: crypto's revenue is dominated by trading and speculation, and the fastest-growing quiet corners are institutional rails like tokenized treasuries. A fair technical translator holds both truths at once.

**Two terms used throughout:** **TVL** ("total value locked") is the dollar value of funds deposited in a chain's apps — the standard size metric. **RWA** ("real-world assets") means traditional assets like US Treasury bonds or credit funds represented as tokens on a blockchain.

## Trading dominates the revenue

The single clearest pattern of 2025–2026: **revenue comes from trading and speculation**, not from "utility" apps.

- **Perps (Hyperliquid):** roughly **$2.7–3.0T** of volume in FY2025 and **>$650M** of 2025 revenue — **#4 in all of crypto** (Bankless, Dec 10, 2025). *Correction per verify pass 2026-11-02:* an earlier draft said "~$633B in 2025"; that **$633B figure is Q1 2026 volume** (perps + spot, per VanEck), not the full year. See [the Hyperliquid deep-dive](perps-hyperliquid.md).
- **Memecoin launchpads (pump.fun):** crossed **$1B cumulative revenue** — **Solana's first billion-dollar app** — reported **March 12, 2026**; **$123M** of that in Q1 2026 alone. See [memecoins and launchpads](memecoins-launchpads.md).
- **Solana's app-revenue engine:** **$292M** dApp revenue in Q1 2026, driven by pump.fun (**$123M**) and Axiom (**$58M**) — per 99Bitcoins/TheStreet; Messari's broader count was **~$342M**, so cite the methodology (verify pass 2026-11-02).

## DeFi today: a down year after a corrected peak

- **Where it stands:** total DeFi TVL **$85.35B** on **Nov 2, 2026** (DefiLlama API; the brief's $85.6B was verified as consistent).
- **The 2026 arc (corrected per verify pass 2026-11-02):** the 2026 peak was **$127.2B on Jan 18, 2026** — not "~$115B," which was a misread of Jan 1's $114.3B. (The all-time high remains ~$177B, Nov 2021.) The peak slid through H1 2026 amid a broad market correction — **Bitcoin down >50% from its Oct 2025 ATH** — and record exploits, reaching a **2026 trough of $68.1B in June**; August 2026 was ~$76B.
- **The year's signature exploit:** on **April 18, 2026**, the **~$292–293M KelpDAO exploit** hit — Aave's TVL fell from **$26.4B to ~$17.9B in 48 hours** and to ~**$14B by month-end** ($14.1B on Apr 30) (verify pass, citing CoinDesk/Galaxy).
- **Major categories:** DEXs (spot trading), lending (Aave dominant), yield/liquid staking and restaking, and derivatives/perps (Hyperliquid dominant, with GMX and Aster).
- **Who grew in 2026:** among top-10 chains, only **TRON (+5%)** and **Hyperliquid (+7%)** grew TVL (BeInCrypto, Jun 25, 2026).

## The quiet giants: RWA and tokenized treasuries

While trading grabs headlines, the institutions build quietly:

- **Total on-chain RWA (excluding stablecoins): ~$38.6–39.2B** as of **Sept 8, 2026** (rwa.xyz data, via stobox.io).
- **Tokenized treasuries** — US Treasury bonds held as tokens — went from **<$1B in early 2024** to **>$10B by January 2026** to **~$14.9–15.1B as of Oct 6, 2026**, across **109 funds on 20+ networks**.
- **The #1 spot flip-flopped through 2026** (per verify pass 2026-11-02): BlackRock's **BUIDL reclaimed #1 at ~$2.8B around Aug 31, 2026**; by early/mid-October 2026, Circle's **USYC led at $2.41B** with BUIDL at ~$2.25B. Never memorize a permanent #1 — check the current date.
- **Chains:** Ethereum still dominates RWA — **~45.8% of RWA value (~$17.1B, Aug 2026)**, down from ~52.9% — but issuers increasingly go multichain: Securitize/Apollo funds deploy on **Avalanche alongside Ethereum, Solana, Aptos, Polygon, Sui, Ink**.
- **Avalanche's slice:** **~$971M "RWA Active AUM"** (Nov 2, 2026, DefiLlama). Named wins: **Apollo tokenized a $50M credit fund on Avalanche in Jan 2026**; **Securitize + Neuberger Berman** launched a tokenized fixed-income fund on Avalanche/Ethereum/Solana/Sui; the **NYSE spent a year testing Avalanche tech for tokenization** (per Ava Labs' president via Cointelegraph, 2026 — original article date uncertain); Avalanche reportedly led **$131.2M in weekly tokenized-stock inflows (Oct 2026)**. The lineage goes back to the 2023 "Evergreen subnet" **Spruce** with T. Rowe Price, WisdomTree, Wellington, and Cumberland.

## Stablecoins and payments

Stablecoins are crypto's most product-market-fit asset — tokens pegged to dollars, used for actual payment.

| Chain (Nov 2, 2026, DefiLlama) | Stablecoin supply |
|---|---|
| Ethereum | $139.5B |
| Tron | $93.7B |
| Solana | $15.0B |
| BSC | $13.1B |
| Hyperliquid | $6.7B |
| Base | $5.1B |
| **Avalanche** | **$1.27B** |
| **Total** | **~$295.5B** ($294.2B by API sum; ~$307B was cited Aug 2026) |

- **Why every chain fights for payments:** Tron earns more fee revenue than most chains purely from settling USDT — and was one of only two top-10 chains to grow TVL in 2026.
- **Regulation unlocked it:** the US **GENIUS Act (signed July 18, 2025)** created the first federal stablecoin framework, unlocking fintech entry.
- **The Tempo signal:** Stripe + Paradigm incubated **Tempo**, a payments-first L1 announced **Sept 4, 2025** (backers include OpenAI, Anthropic, Coupang, Shopify, and major banks); **Tempo mainnet launched March 18, 2026** with an AI-agent payments protocol. By 2026 there are dedicated payments chains everywhere — Tempo, Circle's Arc, Robinhood Chain. **Tempo is not an Avalanche project** — see [The Tempo myth](../avalanche/tempo-myth.md).

## Gaming

- **Off the Grid** (Gunzilla's AAA cyberpunk battle royale) runs on **GUNZ, an Avalanche L1** — mainnet **March 31, 2025**; ~**17.3M testnet wallets by Jul 7, 2025**, but only ~**21K players** migrated to mainnet. It shows blockchain as optional plumbing inside a real game, not marketing.
- **Beam** (Merit Circle DAO's gaming chain, an Avalanche subnet since 2023 with **60+ partner games**) remains Avalanche's main gaming platform; it also expanded to Immutable zkEVM.
- **DeFi Kingdoms — the cautionary tale:** the once-famous game's Avalanche L1, **DFK Chain, officially sunset on August 28, 2026** (announced Jul 31, 2026), with the game migrating back to the C-Chain.

## Curriculum takeaways: three framings

1. **2025–2026 crypto revenue is dominated by trading and speculation** — perps (Hyperliquid: ≈$2.7–3.0T FY2025, corrected per verify pass) and memecoin launchpads (pump.fun: $1B cumulative by Mar 2026) — plus the fast-growing "boring" rails: tokenized treasuries (~$15B, Oct 2026) and stablecoins (~$295B, Nov 2026).
2. **The winning pattern is "app = chain."** Hyperliquid, FIFA, GUNZ, and Tempo all launched purpose-built chains. Avalanche's architecture bet on exactly this future — but the biggest appchain (Hyperliquid) is *not* on Avalanche, FIFA *is* on Avalanche while most finance RWA sits on Ethereum, and the retail middle (memecoins, consumer DeFi) went to Solana and Base.
3. **Avalanche's scoreboard, late 2026:** its strongest-ever institutional pipeline (FIFA/World Cup, NYSE, Apollo) alongside its weakest-ever consumer metrics (TVL rank #12, stablecoins down). **Both are true** — a fair curriculum teaches both. See [the competitive scoreboard](competitive-scoreboard.md).
