## Overview

This is the Module 11 anchor: a dated, honest comparison of Avalanche against Solana, Ethereum + its L2s, and Hyperliquid as of **November 2, 2026**. The scoreboard has two halves, and both are verified: the **criticisms** (TVL and stablecoin lag, the DFK Chain sunset, activity-quality questions) and the **counter-narratives** (L1 customization, ICM, relentless upgrade cadence, and an institutional brand lineup of FIFA, Apollo, and NYSE). The article ends in the only intellectually honest place: what Avalanche still has to prove.

## Versus Ethereum and its L2s

- Ethereum L1 TVL (**$44.4B**) dwarfs Avalanche's — Avalanche sits at ~**1.3%** of it and ~**10%** of Solana's (DefiLlama, Nov 2, 2026).
- **Base** — barely two years old as a consumer chain — holds **$5.99B**, roughly **10x** Avalanche's TVL.
- Ethereum carries **$139.5B in stablecoins (~47% of all)** and remains **Wall Street's default RWA rail** (~45.8% of RWA value, Aug 2026, per rwa.xyz/coinpaprika) — while Avalanche's institutional wins, though real, are a small slice (~$971M RWA Active AUM, Nov 2, 2026).
- One honest counterpoint in Avalanche's favor: **L2 fee economics are commoditized** — ~$182K/24h in chain fees for the L2s versus Solana's $1.03M (MEXC, 2026) — so "Ethereum scaling" isn't automatically winning the revenue war either.

## Avalanche's counter-narratives

1. **L1 customization.** Since the **Etna/Avalanche9000 upgrade (Dec 16, 2024)** — which cut C-Chain fees ~96% and made L1 deployment nearly free (ACP-77; see [L1s after Etna](../avalanche/l1s-after-etna.md)) — the verified count is **~81 active L1s** (VanEck, Feb 25, 2026; Nansen, June 2026: "81 active Avalanche L1s"; Ava Labs said 70+ active in Mar 2026), with **528 total L1s ever created** per Avalanche's explorer. *Per verify pass 2026-11-02:* an earlier "~175 active (Apr 2025)" figure was unsupported and is dropped; counts vary by definition, so always date and source them.
2. **ICM adoption.** Native cross-L1 messaging without external bridges, enhanced by the **Granite upgrade (mainnet Nov 19, 2025)** — see [ICM: cross-chain messaging](../avalanche/icm-crosschain.md).
3. **Relentless upgrade cadence.** **Etna (Dec 16, 2024) → Octane (Apr 8, 2025**; dynamic fees, ACP-176**)** **→ Granite (Nov 19, 2025) → Helicon (Sept 22, 2026**: continuous C-Chain execution, auto-renewing staking, 48-hour minimum staking**)**. No other major chain shipped four named protocol upgrades in this window — see [the upgrade timeline](../avalanche/upgrade-timeline.md).
4. **Enterprise brand.** **FIFA selected Avalanche on May 22, 2025** to build its own chain; **FIFA Collect passed 1M users — reported Sept 17, 2026** (*per verify pass 2026-11-02: not October as first cited*); **World Cup 2026 blockchain ticketing** issued **100K+ ticket rights with $25M+ traded**. Caveat from the verify pass: the 1M-user figure is **platform-reported** — on-chain-verifiable data covers only **85K+ addresses**. Add **Apollo's $50M tokenized credit fund (Jan 2026)**, **NYSE tokenization tests** (announced 2026; original article date uncertain), Securitize/Neuberger, and VanEck framing Avalanche as **"The Institutional Platform"** (2026).

## The honest criticisms

- **TVL/activity lag is real.** $584.0M is ~1.3% of Ethereum's TVL and ~10% of Solana's (Nov 2, 2026). Stablecoin supply **shrank in absolute terms**: Bitquery (stricter methodology) counts **$2.13B (Jan 2025) → ~$998M (Oct 2026)**; DefiLlama shows **$1.27B** (Nov 2, 2026), down ~5% in a week, versus a ~$1.5B ecosystem average in Q1 2026 (Token Terminal).
- **Subnet-era underperformance.** The flagship gaming subnet, **DFK Chain, shut down Aug 28, 2026** (announced Jul 31, 2026) — the cautionary tale of the old model. **Liquidity fragmentation across 80+ L1s** is a recurring critique.
- **Activity-quality questions.** Bitquery's "Who Really Used Avalanche in 2026" investigation and the gap between Nansen's **621K daily active addresses (Q2 2026)** and **86–117K daily active users (Q3 2026)** suggest airdrop-farming inflation — many addresses, fewer actual people.
- **The defenders' rebuttal** (also on the record): consumer-metric comparisons miss the thesis — Avalanche optimized for **institutions issuing their own chains** (FIFA, Apollo, NYSE tests, World Cup ticketing), and usage metrics spiked with the World Cup (**3.5M daily txs**, 2026 high).

## What Avalanche still has to prove

The neutral framing a technical translator should use — neither cheerleading nor doom:

- The technology thesis ("app = chain") **won**: Hyperliquid, FIFA, GUNZ, and Tempo all validated purpose-built chains. Avalanche built the machinery for that future ([L1s after Etna](../avalanche/l1s-after-etna.md)).
- The market position is **unproven**: the biggest appchain isn't on Avalanche, the retail economy lives on Solana/Base, and Avalanche's own consumer metrics are at their weakest even as its institutional pipeline is at its strongest.
- As one neutral summary put it (247wallst.com, **Sept 30, 2026**): **"Avalanche has to prove what Solana already did"** — build a self-reinforcing consumer economy on its rails, the way Solana did, while the institutional chains it hosts mature.
- Fair answer to "is it keeping up?": *"Both things are true at once — the strongest institutional pipeline Avalanche has ever had, and the weakest consumer metrics it has ever had. Here are the dated numbers on both sides."* Then use the tables above.
